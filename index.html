Event Reminder
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes the Android app Event Reminder, package com.littlesteps.eventreminder, published under the LittleSteps Lab developer name. Questions and privacy requests: littlesteps.surat@gmail.com.

The practices below describe version 1.0.5. Earlier distributed versions may differ in their advertising controls, contact-linked deletion behavior and treatment of unknown years.

What the app does

Event Reminder stores birthdays, anniversaries and other yearly occasions, schedules notifications, displays home-screen widgets, and helps you prepare wishes. The app is free to use, has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and is supported by Google AdMob bottom-banner advertising on the main list. The app currently does not display full-screen advertising. LittleSteps Lab does not provide an Event Reminder account, a custom contact-sync service or an online database for your events.

Events and photos stored on your device

Names, event types, dates, optional phone/email details, notes, reminder preferences, selected photos and message templates are stored in the app's local database or private app storage. These event contents are not sent to LittleSteps Lab or to the advertising network. Event data remains in app storage until you delete or replace it, clear app storage, or uninstall. App-managed temporary photo files are removed after processing. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled. In version 1.0.5, a date stored with 1900 as its year is treated as having no known year and is not used to calculate or display an age.

Photo selection uses an Android system picker; the app does not request broad photo-library or storage permission. Selecting a photo imports a resized copy. Contact photos may also be read when contact access is granted. Removing an imported image from Event Reminder does not delete the original image from your gallery or address book.

Optional device-contacts integration

You can use the app with manually entered events without granting contacts access. In version 1.0.5, active contact linking is limited to Google-backed contacts already available through the Android contact service. With Contacts permission, the app can read names, contact links, birthdays, anniversaries and custom dates, phone/email details and available contact photos to create and maintain linked local events.

The app may refresh linked information when it opens, while it is open after the Android contact service reports a change, and during background work scheduled by Android. Refresh timing depends on Android's scheduling and device conditions. Existing events imported by earlier versions from device-only or SIM sources are retained as independent local events rather than silently removed.

Event Reminder does not access Gmail, ask you to sign in to Google, request Google account credentials, or operate a custom contacts server. Permission to edit contacts is used only after an explicit action to save or delete the selected birthday, anniversary or custom date of a Google-backed device contact. Saving adds or updates only that selected date entry. Deleting a Google-linked event removes only that selected date entry and its linked local event; it does not delete the person or change other contact details. Deleting a local-only event does not change Android contacts.

Google account contact synchronization is controlled through Android and Google account settings. A contact change made on the device may reach Google Contacts and other signed-in devices according to those settings, network availability and the account provider's timing; synchronization may be delayed or unavailable. If you revoke Contacts permission, previously imported local information is not automatically erased. Delete the relevant local events or clear app storage if you wish to remove it.

Notifications and widgets

Notification permission is used for event reminders and snooze. Optional Alarms & reminders special access permits more precise timing; without it the app uses approximate scheduling. Android battery restrictions can affect delivery. You can disable reminders or notification permission.

Notifications and home-screen widgets can display names and dates to someone who can see your device. Review Android's notification visibility settings, and remove widgets if this information should not be visible.

Advertising, consent and privacy options

Version 1.0.5 is an advertising-supported service. When the main event list is used, the app automatically begins requesting a Google AdMob bottom banner, subject to applicable Google privacy requirements. The app currently does not display full-screen advertising, including after event saves or on app launch. Notifications and background contact work do not display ads. Advertising depends on consent status, network access and ad availability; a missing or failed banner does not block the app's event and reminder features. Google supplies the advertisement content and its interaction controls.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads SDK 25.5.0 and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0. The app does not ask a custom age question or infer your age from saved events. As a conservative configuration, every ad request receives child-protective treatment and a general-audience (G) content limit, and the publisher setting disables advertising personalization. These settings do not determine the user's age, make the app child-directed, or make advertising data-free.

Google UMP determines whether a privacy message or privacy choices are required for the user. Where required, the app presents Google's configured message before requesting advertising. Menu > Ad privacy choices opens Google's privacy options when they are required and available. These choices affect the advertising and data processing that Google permits. If applicable privacy requirements do not permit an ad request, the event and reminder features remain available.

Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), product and ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and applicable device or signed-in account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. UMP also processes privacy-message status and choices. The child treatment prevents the Android advertising ID from being sent with those ad requests, but other identifiers and network data can still be processed. Privacy choices and non-personalized or limited ad serving can reduce processing without eliminating all SDK data handling. No event name, birthday, note, contact detail or selected image is supplied as an ad targeting input. The app does not request Android location permission.

Google documents encryption in transit for its advertising SDK. Google and advertising service providers control retention of the data they process; LittleSteps Lab does not specify or guarantee a retention period for those services. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. Android advertising settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Changing a privacy choice does not necessarily erase data already processed by Google or another advertising provider.

Backups, wishes and external apps

Exported JSON backups contain readable names, dates, phone/email details, notes and embedded selected photos. They are not password-encrypted by this app. You choose the storage provider or destination through Android's file picker or sharesheet. That destination may store or upload the backup under its own policies. Share only with trusted destinations and delete exported copies separately when no longer needed.

Calls, SMS, email and shared wishes open other apps for your chosen action. Those apps receive the selected recipient details and/or message text and have their own privacy practices. Event Reminder does not silently send messages or place calls. Gift search opens a browser with generic occasion terms, not a person's name, event date or contact details. External websites, advertisements and apps may process additional data under their own policies.

Deletion and your choices

Delete events through their detail dialog. In version 1.0.5, deleting a Google-linked event also deletes only its selected birthday, anniversary or custom date entry from the Google-backed device contact. It never deletes the person or unrelated contact fields. Deleting a local-only event does not change contacts. Whether and when that date deletion reaches Google Contacts or another signed-in device depends on Android and Google account sync settings, network access and provider availability.

The app may retain a local record of a removed linked event to avoid importing it again. To remove all app-private events, those records, imported photos, templates and preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > Event Reminder > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall. Clearing storage or uninstalling does not delete whole source contacts, original gallery photos, external backups, messages or data already processed by Google, and it does not restore a contact date previously deleted through an explicit linked event deletion. Contact the relevant providers for their deletion options.

There is no app account to delete. Contact us for questions about this policy. If you email support, your address and the information you send are processed in our support mailbox to respond to your request; avoid sending private backups or contact lists. Support emails are retained while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion, subject to those obligations.

Audience

The current Google Play target-audience declaration is ages 13 and older. Version 1.0.5 does not ask users to confirm their age and applies child-protective advertising treatment to everyone. This setting does not establish that the app is designed for toddlers or change the Play target-audience declaration. Follow applicable parental-consent requirements. Do not use the app to publish someone else's private contact information. Local event entries can include dates for people of different ages; these dates are never used to determine the app user's age.

Policy changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes requiring new consent will be disclosed before the new processing starts. The installed app includes an offline copy of its policy and a link to the public policy when configured for release.
