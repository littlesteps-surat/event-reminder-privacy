Event Reminder
Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy describes the Android app Event Reminder, package com.littlesteps.eventreminder, published by LittleSteps Lab. Questions and privacy requests: littlesteps.surat@gmail.com.

What the app does

Event Reminder stores birthdays, anniversaries and other yearly occasions, schedules notifications, displays home-screen widgets, and helps you prepare wishes. The app is free to use, has no in-app purchases or subscriptions, and supports optional Google AdMob bottom-banner advertising. Full-screen advertising is disabled in the current default configuration. There is no Event Reminder account, custom contact-sync server or developer-operated event database.

Events and photos stored on your device

Names, event types, dates, optional phone/email details, notes, reminder preferences, selected photos and message templates are stored in the app's local SQLite database or private app storage. These event contents are not sent to LittleSteps Lab or to the advertising network. Event data remains in app storage until you delete or replace it, clear app storage, or uninstall. App-managed temporary photo files are removed after processing. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled.

Photo selection uses an Android system picker; the app does not request broad photo-library or storage permission. Selecting a photo imports a resized copy. Contact photos may also be read when contact access is granted. Removing an imported image from Event Reminder does not delete the original image from your gallery or address book.

Optional address-book synchronization

You can use the app with manually entered events without granting contacts access. If you enable automatic contact synchronization and grant the Android contacts permission, the app reads contact names, birthday and anniversary/custom date entries, contact links, phone/email details and available contact photos to create and maintain linked local events.

Synchronization runs when the app opens, when the address book changes while the app is open, and during background checks scheduled by Android, even when you are not using the app. Android may delay these checks. Source changes and deletions update linked events. An ambiguous changed source with local customizations may be preserved as a separate local event to prevent data loss.

The app reads contacts already on this device. Android and your contact account provider, such as Google, manage their own account synchronization. Event Reminder does not access your Gmail inbox, request Google account credentials or upload your contacts to a custom server. In version 1.0.1 and later, adding or changing a contact-linked event date can update that selected birthday, anniversary or custom-date entry directly in your address book after you choose Save and grant contact-editing permission. It does not change other contacts or their unrelated fields. Android and the contact provider manage any resulting Google-backed synchronization. Opening the full contact editor remains available for other contact changes. Saving a contact-linked date enables automatic address-book event synchronization, including Android-scheduled background checks, as explained in the date editor. You can turn it off through the app menu.

You can turn synchronization off in the app menu or revoke Contacts permission in Android settings. Previously imported local information is not automatically erased by revoking permission; delete those events or clear app storage if you wish to remove it. Deleting a linked event in this app does not itself delete the source contact.

Notifications and widgets

Notification permission is used for event reminders and snooze. Optional Alarms & reminders special access permits more precise timing; without it the app uses approximate scheduling. Android battery restrictions can affect delivery. You can disable reminders or notification permission.

Notifications and home-screen widgets can display names and dates to someone who can see your device. Review Android's notification visibility settings, and remove widgets if this information should not be visible.

Advertising and privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, a banner can appear at the bottom of the main screen. Full-screen interstitial advertising is disabled in the current default configuration, including after event saves and on app launch. Notifications and background contact synchronization do not display ads. If no advertisement is available, the app continues without waiting for one. Advertising providers supply the banner content and its interaction controls.

The app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). In version 1.0.2 and later, banners load automatically on the event-list screen when advertising is on. Advertising is on by default for new installations, and an existing explicit choice to turn ads off is kept. You can change this through Menu > Advertising without losing the event and reminder features. There is no custom age question. Because the app does not determine your age, every ad request receives child-protective treatment, a general-audience content limit and disabled advertising personalization. These protections do not mean advertising is data-free or that your age has been determined.

Versions 1.0 and 1.0.1 required a locally stored broad age choice, 13-17 or 18+, before enabling ads, through Advertising and privacy choices. They did not collect an exact age or birth date. Their teen option applied conservative under-age-of-consent protections.

Advertising protection flags and your consent or opt-out selections are passed to Google's SDKs and services to apply those protections and choices. The app does not send an exact age, birth date or event contents for this purpose, and never uses a saved contact's age as your age.

When advertising is on, UMP contacts Google's services to determine applicable privacy messages and choices. Where required, the app presents Google's configured consent form before requesting ads. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Menu > Manage advertising consent lets you revisit those choices. Ads are not requested unless UMP permits requests. If no permissible consent status is available, reminders remain usable without ads.

Google's advertising SDK can collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information and applicable app/device identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Version 1.0.2's child-protected ad requests disable transmission of the Android advertising ID. Earlier versions could use that identifier depending on age and consent settings. Privacy settings limit processing but do not remove all SDK data handling. No event name, birthday, note, contact detail or selected image is supplied as an ad targeting input. The app does not request Android location permission.

Google documents encryption in transit for its advertising SDK. Google and advertising service providers control retention of the data they process; LittleSteps Lab does not specify or guarantee a retention period for those services. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information. Android advertising settings let you reset or delete your advertising ID. Changing consent or disabling ads does not necessarily erase previously processed third-party data.

Backups, wishes and external apps

Exported JSON backups contain readable names, dates, phone/email details, notes and embedded selected photos. They are not password-encrypted by this app. You choose the storage provider or destination through Android's file picker or sharesheet. That destination may store or upload the backup under its own policies. Share only with trusted destinations and delete exported copies separately when no longer needed.

Calls, SMS, email and shared wishes open other apps for your chosen action. Those apps receive the selected recipient details and/or message text and have their own privacy practices. Event Reminder does not silently send messages or place calls. Gift search opens a browser with generic occasion terms, not a person's name, event date or contact details. External websites, advertisements and apps may process additional data under their own policies.

Deletion and your choices

Delete events through their detail dialog. Contact-linked event exclusions may be retained locally to prevent deleted entries from being re-imported. To remove all app-private events, exclusions, imported photos, templates and preferences, use Android Settings > Apps > Event Reminder > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall. This does not delete source contacts, original gallery photos, external backups, messages or data already processed by Google. Contact those providers for their deletion options.

There is no app account to delete. Contact us for questions about this policy. If you email support, your address and the information you send are processed in our support mailbox to respond to your request; avoid sending private backups or contact lists. Support emails are retained while needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations; you may request deletion, subject to those obligations.

Audience

The current Google Play target-audience declaration is ages 13 and older. Version 1.0.2 does not ask users to confirm their age and applies child-protective advertising treatment to everyone. This setting does not establish that the app is designed for toddlers or change the Play target-audience declaration. Follow applicable parental-consent requirements. Do not use the app to publish someone else's private contact information. Local event entries can include dates for people of different ages; these dates are never used to determine the app user's age.

Policy changes

We will update this page when the app's data practices change and revise the date above. Material changes requiring new consent will be disclosed before the new processing starts. The installed app includes an offline copy of its policy and a link to the public policy when configured for release.
