Contacts and external services

Contact integration is optional. Version 1.0.5 reads only Google-backed contacts already available through the Android contact service after you grant Contacts permission. Existing events imported by earlier versions from device-only or SIM sources remain independent local events. The app does not access Gmail, ask you to sign in to Google or provide cloud backup of its event database.

After an explicit action and permission to edit contacts, saving a linked event can add or update only its selected birthday, anniversary or custom date. Deleting a Google-linked event also deletes only that selected date entry from the device contact; it does not delete the person or other contact details. Deleting a local-only event does not change contacts. Android and Google account settings control contact synchronization, which may be delayed or unavailable depending on network, device and provider conditions. Version 1.0.5 treats a date stored with 1900 as its year as having no known year rather than calculating an age from it.
